What is a Budu Guna Counter?

A Budu Guna Counter (also known as a Navaguna clicker or Itipi So counter) is a dedicated counting tool used to tally recitations of the Nine Supreme Virtues of the Buddha (නව අරහාදී බුදු ගුණ). In Theravada Buddhist tradition across Sri Lanka, Thailand, Myanmar, and worldwide, chanting these nine attributes cultivates supreme mental tranquility, deep mindfulness (Buddhānussati), spiritual protection (Paritta), and immense merit.

Traditional chanters use physical 108-bead wooden or bodhi seed malas. This online tally counter replicates the sacred rhythm of physical mala beads directly on your smartphone, tablet, or desktop with offline local memory and tactile feedback.

The Nine Supreme Virtues of the Buddha (Navaguna - නව අරහාදී බුදු ගුණ)

The Itipi So Bhagavā gāthā encapsulates the nine transcendent qualities realized by Gautama Buddha. You can track each virtue individually in our Advance Multi-Counter mode or chant them as a complete 108 mala cycle in Single Zen mode:

01

Araham (අරහං)

The Worthy One

Totally freed from all mental defilements (kilesas), worthy of supreme homage and offerings by deities and humankind.

02

Sammā-Sambuddho (සම්මා සම්බුද්ධෝ)

Fully Self-Enlightened

Possessing direct, unassisted realization of the Four Noble Truths and the true nature of reality without any teacher.

03

Vijjā-Caraṇa-Sampanno (විජ්ජාචරණ සම්පන්නෝ)

Endowed with Wisdom & Virtue

Equipped with the threefold/eightfold higher spiritual knowledge (vijjā) and flawless ethical conduct (caraṇa).

04

Sugato (සුගතෝ)

The Well-Gone One

Traveled the Noble Eightfold Path gracefully and reached the unshakeable bliss of Nibbāna; speaker of only beneficial truth.

05

Lokavidū (ලෝකවිදූ)

Knower of the Worlds

Comprehends the nature, origin, and cessation of all realms of sentient beings, the inanimate universe, and the mental world.

06

Anuttaro Purisa-Damma-Sārathi (අනුත්තරෝ පුරිසදම්මසාරථී)

Incomparable Leader of Beings

The unmatched spiritual trainer capable of taming fierce beings, kings, and celestial spirits with boundless compassion and skillful means.

07

Satthā Deva-Manussānam (සත්ථා දේවමනුස්සානං)

Teacher of Gods & Humans

The supreme teacher who guides gods, devas, and human beings across the ocean of suffering to eternal peace.

08

Buddho (බුද්ධෝ)

The Awakened One

Fully awakened from the deep slumber of ignorance, who awakens other sentient beings to liberating Dhamma.

09

Bhagavā (භගවා)

The Blessed Lord

Possessing glorious spiritual accomplishments, merits, and the supreme capacity to analyze and teach the Dhamma.

Why 108 Beads? The Meaning Behind Buddhist Mala Counts

A standard Buddhist counting rosary (japa mala) contains 108 beads. In Buddhist numerology and psychology:

  • 6 Senses: Sight, sound, smell, taste, touch, and consciousness.
  • 3 Feelings: Pleasant, unpleasant, and neutral (6 × 3 = 18).
  • 2 Origins: Arising internally or externally (18 × 2 = 36).
  • 3 Times: Past, present, and future (36 × 3 = 108 defilements / kilesas).

By reciting a sacred gāthā or virtue 108 times, the chanter purifies these 108 potential mental hindrances. Our tool features built-in milestone tracking for 108 repetitions, with automatic Lap counters (Lap 1, Lap 2, Lap 3...) as you progress through thousands of chants.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the counter remember my tallies?

All counts, custom goals, and step increments are automatically saved to your browser's local memory (localStorage). Your data remains safe even if you refresh or close your browser.

Can I export and back up my chant counts?

Yes. Open the Settings drawer (top right icon) and click "Export Data (JSON)". You can save your file to your phone or computer, or restore it on any device via "Import Backup".

How do I use keyboard shortcuts on desktop?

Press Spacebar or ↑ to increment count, ↓ to decrement, R to reset, M to toggle between Single and Advance mode, and B for Blind Mode.

What is Blind Mode?

Blind Mode dims all icons and buttons, turning your phone or desktop screen into a single seamless tap surface. This allows you to close your eyes, focus on breath and contemplation, and tap anywhere to count.
